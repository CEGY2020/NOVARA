User Guide

Find a site or system

Choose RHW (Restaurant Hot Water), DHW (Domestic Hot Water), HVAC, or Pool from the top menu. Open Portfolio for shortcuts, or select Sites or Systems directly. Application views show records with a matching system type; unclassified records remain available under View all applications.

Owners and Management Companies are shared records. Equipment / Assets opens system records, where equipment counts and system details are stored. Alerts opens the existing shared portfolio alert page.

Customers and contacts

  1. Open Customers and search by company, site, contact, phone, or email.
  2. Select a company to view its linked sites and contacts.
  3. Use the Edit and Save controls to update a record.

The Contacts shortcut opens a searchable list of customer contacts.

Track leads

Open Leads to review follow-ups and the sales pipeline. Select a lead to view or update its progress.

Upload customer forms

For administrators, open Account, then Master Data Upload. Choose a customer form from your files and review the upload results before continuing.

Account tools

Account contains your existing role-specific dashboard, reports, utility data, and settings. Available tools depend on your account role. Use Logout beside your profile to sign out.
