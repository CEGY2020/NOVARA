Letter of Intent

This Letter of Intent confirms the customer's interest in continuing the evaluation of the energy efficiency opportunity at the property.

The purpose of the next phase is to obtain and review the information required to develop a more detailed energy and economic analysis. This may include natural-gas usage information through UtilityAPI or other authorized utility records, verification of existing equipment make, model and ratings, operating information, and contractor/pricing information applicable to the proposed project.

This Letter of Intent is intended to authorize continued evaluation and information gathering. It is not a final construction contract or final project approval. Final scope, pricing, savings projections, subscription terms and implementation requirements will be presented separately for customer review and approval.
