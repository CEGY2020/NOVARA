NOVARA DHW Master Contractor Installation Agreement

DRAFT FOR BUSINESS AND LEGAL REVIEW

This Master Contractor Installation Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Advanced Energy Management, Inc. (“AEM”) and (“Contractor”). It establishes the general terms under which Contractor may perform physical installation work for NOVARA projects. Individual projects will be authorized by a separate Project Work Order / Installation Authorization.

1. Scope and Roles

Contractor is responsible for the physical installation and workmanship within Contractor’s licensed and authorized trade scope, including only the work identified in an approved Project Work Order. AEM retains responsibility for Optima ProLink software, analytics, control strategy, remote monitoring, optimization and final commissioning acceptance. Contractor shall not alter AEM software, control logic or programming except as expressly authorized in writing by AEM.

2. Project Work Orders

No site work is authorized until AEM issues or approves a written Project Work Order identifying the property, equipment, installation scope, price, schedule and any site-specific requirements. Changes require written approval before additional work is performed.

3. Licensing, Permits and Insurance

Contractor shall maintain all licenses, registrations and insurance required for its work and shall obtain permits and inspections assigned to Contractor in the applicable Work Order. Contractor shall provide evidence upon request.

4. Installation Standards

Contractor shall perform work in a professional manner, follow applicable codes and manufacturer instructions, protect existing equipment and property, and immediately report conditions that could affect safety, performance, scope or price.

5. Documentation and Commissioning

Contractor shall provide required equipment/nameplate photographs, installation photographs, model and serial information, test results and completion documentation. Contractor shall cooperate with AEM commissioning. Installation completion does not constitute AEM commissioning acceptance.

6. Pricing, Invoicing and Changes

Pricing is established by each Work Order. Contractor shall not exceed the authorized amount without a written change authorization. Invoices must identify the applicable project and Work Order and include required completion documentation.

7. Warranty and Correction of Work

Contractor is responsible for its workmanship and for correcting installation defects attributable to its work. AEM is responsible for its software and services as provided under separate customer or program terms. Equipment manufacturer warranties remain subject to their applicable terms.

8. Independent Contractor

Contractor is an independent contractor and is not an employee, agent, partner or joint venturer of AEM. Contractor is responsible for its personnel, payroll, taxes, supervision and trade practices.

9. Confidentiality and AEM Technology

Contractor shall protect non-public AEM technical, customer and project information. No ownership or license to AEM software, algorithms, control logic, analytics, documentation or other intellectual property is transferred except the limited access necessary to perform an authorized Work Order.

10. Safety, Damage and Compliance

Contractor controls and is responsible for the safety of its personnel and its means and methods of installation. Contractor shall comply with applicable laws and promptly report property damage, incidents or unsafe conditions related to its work.

11. Termination

Either party may discontinue future Work Orders. Termination does not eliminate obligations for authorized work already performed, confidentiality, correction of defective work, or other provisions intended to survive.

12. Final Terms

This draft is intended to establish the operational structure for AEM’s contractor program. Governing law, indemnity, insurance limits, dispute resolution, payment timing and other legal provisions must be approved by AEM before this form is used as an executed contract.
