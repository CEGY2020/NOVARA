Master Data Upload

Import permanent NOVARA companies and sites without creating Leads.

Import order

1. Companies   2. Sites   3. Contacts

Leads are never created by this tool. Ambiguous Owner / Management Company records are skipped for review rather than guessed.

1. Companies

Upload a CSV containing CompanyName plus CompanyType. CompanyType must be Owner or Management Company. Rows marked for review are not imported.

2. Sites

Upload the cleaned Sites CSV. Existing sites are matched by normalized site name + address + city and will be skipped rather than duplicated.

3. Contacts

NOVARA does not yet have a separate permanent Contacts object. Uploading the Contacts CSV here validates and stages the rows only; it does not convert them into Leads or discard them.
